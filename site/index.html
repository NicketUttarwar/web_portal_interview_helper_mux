From the source to the master

Five passes take the source audio and hand back a finished episode.

  1. HearWe transcribe all of the source.
  2. ReadWe find which segments to keep and which to discard.
  3. ShapeWe shape it into a story, with an optional synthetic interviewer voice to weave it together.
  4. BuildWe add the music.
  5. ShipWe push the final master.

Zero Shot Media takes a raw interview and builds the master. The story is chosen for this tape. The audio work stays on the machine.

Language models decide the shape and write only from what was said. Local models hear the tape, speak the new lines, and write the music. Speech stays above the bed.

How analysis works

After the words are on the page, the run reads the tape for what a first listener should keep.

See the run
  • 01Words

    Heard, then taken as said

    Local speech models write the words and split the speakers. Full-auto signs that page itself and carries those words into the keep.
  • 02Nuggets

    The stretches worth keeping

    Analysis looks for passionate, information-dense passages that the surrounding talk had buried.
  • 03Cuts

    In and out on the idea

    Each keep gets cut points that are easy to hear and still hold the thought together.
  • 04Shape

    A plan for this tape only

    Research reads the run in waves. Shape then picks the story. No show is forced into one template.
  • 05Voice

    New lines from old words

    Bridges recombine what that speaker already said. They do not invent a claim that was never spoken.

How a run moves

The packages under the five passes. Speech, speakers, voice, and music run on this machine.

  • Whisper

    mlx-audio

    ffmpeg turns the upload into 16-bit WAV. mlx-community/whisper-large-v3-turbo then writes every word, with timings. DeepFilterNet3 can quiet the room first. If that stack is unavailable, ffmpeg afftdn is the fallback.

  • Sortformer

    Four speakers

    mlx-community/diar_sortformer_4spk-v1-fp32 marks who is talking. The model covers up to four voices, and it runs on MLX beside Whisper.

  • OpenAI

    gpt-5.6-terra

    gpt-5.6-terra is the flagship for the reading, the shape, and the bridge lines. Smaller calls use gpt-4o-mini. gpt-4o conducts the walk. The cover is three gpt-image-2 candidates.

  • Chatterbox

    ResembleAI

    ResembleAI/chatterbox speaks the optional interviewer lines in a voice already on the tape. If synthesis fails, mlx-audio is the fallback voice.

  • MusicGen

    Large, then down

    Theme beds start at facebook/musicgen-large, then musicgen-medium, then musicgen-small. Melody beds use musicgen-melody. MMAudio is the backup. The mix keeps the talk above the bed in master.wav.
